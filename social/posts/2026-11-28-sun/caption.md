# Sun and shade over the Dolomites, 28 Nov

A Map trick post (`docs/social.md`, Series), the first sun reel. Just for the look.

- **Format**: reel, 9:16, 12 s (`reel-9x16.mp4`). No story: it is scheduled, and stories cannot be
- **Post**: Sat 28 Nov 2026, scheduled in the app the evening before (about 10:00)
- **Made with**: `node scripts/social.mjs sun --mode day --place dolomites --date 2026-11-28 --out social/posts/2026-11-28-sun`, Fri 27 Nov. Sella and Langkofel from 15 minutes before sunrise (07:32) to 20 minutes after sunset (16:29); the sun is 22° high at noon. Clear sky assumed, no trees or buildings.
