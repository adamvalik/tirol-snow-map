# Spike: the name and the domain

Date: Nov 22–23, 2026. Scope: find a name and a domain before the app gets an Instagram account and a public launch (#32). The decision is in [`roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md) (Decisions and Name).

## Verdict

**Cima Map**, on **cimamap.com** (and cimamap.app), handle **`@cimamap`**. The descriptive line under it: "Cima Map – 3D snow and terrain map of South Tyrol".

*Cima* is Italian for peak or summit. The app is built in Italy, and the word is easy to explain. "Map" is always part of the name: it says what the app is and sets it apart from other uses of *Cima* (see Cima in detail).

## What the name had to do

- Short, easy to say and spell in English, German and Italian, and not offensive in any of them.
- Tied to neither snow nor the region: the app is used in summer too (3D terrain, sun and shade, via ferratas), and the area may grow to the Euregio (#40) or beyond. The descriptive line carries the region and the snow focus and is cheap to change.
- Free as a domain (.com or .app) and as a handle on Instagram, TikTok, Threads and YouTube.
- No clash with an app or brand in outdoor, maps or weather, and no EU trade mark.
- Reads well in a link (`cimamap.com/?…#…`) and on the share picture next to the QR code.

## How it was checked

| Check | How | Reliability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| .com, .app | The registries' RDAP (Verisign, Google Registry): "not found" means not registered | Reliable |
| .eu, .it, .at | DNS only: no record for the name means very likely free | Confirm at a registrar |
| .tirol | The registry's RDAP | Reliable |
| YouTube | The public `youtube.com/@handle` page | Good |
| TikTok | The public profile page | Indicative |
| Instagram, Threads | Need a login: checked by hand by the owner for the winner | Good, for the winner only |
| EU trade marks | EUIPO eSearch plus, by hand by the owner (TMview rejects automated queries) | Exact matches only |
| Apps and brands | Web search for the name with outdoor, maps, weather | Not the app stores' own search |

Prices are not shown by the registries. Typical at Cloudflare Registrar or Porkbun: .com about €10 a year, .app about €12–15, unless a name is premium. Compare renewal prices on tld-list.com.

## How it went

Three rounds of brainstorming, then a deep look at the finalists.

1. **Short brand words** (the ideas in the roadmap and more). Almost every short dictionary word is taken as .com, .eu and .it; most are taken as .app too. Kamm (ridge) was the first pick, as the only one with a free .app, and was dropped by the owner: in English it sounds like "cum".
2. **Italian words, around *Cima*.** The owner liked how Cima sounds. Cima, Cimalta and Cimetta were looked at closely (below).
3. **Back to the start, Tirol.** tirolsnowmap and tirolmap are free on every ending. Dropped for the Tirol/Tyrol/Tirolo spelling, and because "Tirol" for South Tyrol reads as political to some Italian speakers. The Land Tirol also guards the name; its logo was never an option.
4. **Around "map".** Blends of a map word (map, mappa, Karte, atlas, topo) and a mountain word. The finalists were Alpmap, Cimamap and Alp Snow Map. Alpmap fell because alpmap.com runs a similar idea and `@alpmap` is taken on Instagram.

Cima Map won: every domain ending and every handle free, no EU trade mark for "CIMAMAP" or "CIMA MAP", and it covers summer and any area.

## Cima in detail

- **Meaning**: peak or summit in Italian; "top" in Spanish and Portuguese. In Czech, Polish and Russian, *zima* means winter, a harmless coincidence. Nothing offensive found in English, German or Italian.
- **Saying it**: Italian CHEE-ma. German readers tend to say TSEE-ma, English readers SEE-ma or KEE-ma. None is awkward; the Italian one is the one to use in videos.
- **Domains** (Nov 23): cima.app (since Jul 2022) and cima.com are taken. cimamap.com, cimamap.app, cimamap.eu, cimamap.it and cimamap.at are free.
- **Handles** (Nov 23): `@cima` is taken on YouTube and TikTok. `@cimamap` is free on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.
- **Other uses of the name**:
  - *Cima*, an AI bouldering progress tracker (getcima.app, since Nov 2025). Same outdoor field, different purpose. "Map" in the name keeps them apart.
  - *CIMA Research Foundation* (Savona): hydro-meteorology research, runs myDewetra for Italy's Civil Protection. Professional, not a consumer app.
  - Smaller: Cimalp (French outdoor clothing), Cims (a hiking app), CIMA the accountants' institute (it fills search results for "cima" alone).
- **Trade marks**: EUIPO, "CIMAMAP" and "CIMA MAP": none (Nov 23). A search for "CIMA" alone in classes 9 and 42 is the wider check.
- **Close variants looked at**: Cimalta ("high peak", cimalta.app free) and Cimetta ("little peak", cimetta.app free). Three syllables each, and Cimetta sounds small for a map.

## Using the name

- Write **Cima Map**, two words, both capitalised. In addresses and handles: `cimamap`.
- The descriptive line goes with the name where there is room, for example the page title, link previews and the share picture.
- The repository stays *tirol-snow-map*.
